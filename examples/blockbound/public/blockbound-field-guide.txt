BLOCKBOUND — YOUR FIRST DAY
An unofficial fan-made field guide.

1. FOLLOW THE LIGHT
Start in Sunmeadow at sunrise. Mark your spawn with a small lantern, then walk toward the river. The village paths are the safest way to learn the lay of the land.

2. PACK FOR THE JOURNEY
Bring a pickaxe, a stack of blocks, food, and one torch per floor of your base. Leave room in your inventory for discoveries.

3. BUILD SOMETHING SMALL
Your first home does not need to be grand. Make a shelter, a door, a bed, and one window facing east. A memorable world begins with a memorable first block.

4. READ EACH BIOME
Sunmeadow: fertile ground and gentle slopes.
Whisperwood: dense canopy, rich timber, glowing spores.
Crystal Hollow: beautiful, quiet, and easy to get lost in.
Emberwaste: bright lava, dark basalt, and no room for mistakes.
The Beyond: floating islands, ancient stone, and an impossible sky.

5. LEAVE IT BETTER
Repair a path. Light a bridge. Plant a grove. The best adventures are the ones other players can still find after you leave.
